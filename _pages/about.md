I am a 1st year Planetary Science PhD Student in the Earth, Planetary, and Space Science department at UCLA. My research focuses on studying the atmospheric composition of exoplanets, with a particular focus on the nature of atmospheres on rocky worlds.

I previously completed my B.Sc. with Honours in Astronomy and Astrophysics at the University of Calgary in 2023, and my M.Sc. in Physics at the University of Montréal in 2026. My [M.Sc. thesis](https://umontreal.scholaris.ca/items/7adf831a-38b0-4d41-baab-95bbc3c9964b) focused on the atmospheric characterization of rocky exoplanets using emission spectroscopy, and discussed the potential detection of a volatile-rich atmosphere surrounding the highly-irradiated lava world TOI-431 b.

Much of my current research at UCLA aims to further probe the diversity and complexity of atmospheres on terrestrial exoplanets. However, I am interested in a number of other subjects in the field of exoplaneteology, including orbital dynamics, exogeology, and habitability. In fact, I'm currently leading a study on constraining the composition and dynamical history of WASP-107 b from the planet's JWST eclipse timing and spectrum. Check out the Research tab above to learn more about my work.
